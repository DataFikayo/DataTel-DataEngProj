-- Four business questions answered directly from dw_user_analytics.
-- Query 1: Top 10 customers by lifetime revenue
-- Query 2: Customer segmentation by revenue bands
-- Query 3: Churn risk flagging by session and revenue thresholds
-- Query 4: Revenue vs usage mismatch detection


SELECT 
  customer_id,
  customer_name,
  total_revenue
FROM `dataengtest-496013.datatel.dw_user_analytics`
ORDER BY total_revenue DESC
LIMIT 10;

SELECT
  customer_id,
  customer_name,
  total_revenue,
  CASE
    WHEN total_revenue > 5000000 THEN 'High Value'
    WHEN total_revenue > 1000000 THEN 'Mid Value'
    ELSE 'Low Value'
  END AS customer_segment
FROM `dataengtest-496013.datatel.dw_user_analytics`
ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;

-------------------------

Justification:
The current churn rule flags any customer with fewer than 5 sessions AND less than ₦1,000 in revenue as High Risk. The weakness is that a brand new customer who registered yesterday would also have 0 sessions and 0 revenue — they are not at risk of churning, they just haven't started yet. To fix this I would add an account age condition: only flag customers whose account is older than 30 days but still shows low activity. This makes the rule target genuinely inactive customers rather than new ones.

SELECT
  customer_id,
  customer_name,
  total_sessions,
  total_revenue,
  CASE
    WHEN total_sessions < 5 AND total_revenue < 1000 THEN 'High Risk'
    ELSE 'Active'
  END AS churn_risk
FROM `dataengtest-496013.datatel.dw_user_analytics`
ORDER BY churn_risk DESC;
